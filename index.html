Weather Forecasts Anywhere — Even Off-Grid

Sat-Text lets you receive real-time NOAA weather forecasts through satellite texting. If your phone supports satellite messaging, you can text us your GPS coordinates and instantly receive a detailed weather forecast — even with zero cell signal.

Satellite Text Compatible

iPhones and modern devices with satellite texting can send coordinates to our number, allowing full off-grid access to forecasts.

Powered by NOAA

Forecasts come directly from the National Weather Service for accurate, official data.

Works Without Service

Because satellite texting bypasses traditional cell networks, Sat-Text works during outages, backcountry travel, storms, and emergencies.
